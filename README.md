# GuestPulse AI

### Resolve Before They Review.

GuestPulse AI is an AI-powered hospitality complaint intelligence platform designed to help hotels understand, prioritize and respond to guest complaints before they become negative experiences.

## Problem

Hotels receive guest complaints through multiple channels such as reception desks, calls, messages and feedback forms.

Important complaints can be delayed, misrouted or left unresolved.

## Our Solution

GuestPulse AI analyzes guest complaints and provides:

- Smart complaint understanding
- Sentiment detection
- Urgency detection
- Guest Risk Score
- Department routing
- Priority classification
- Complaint escalation
- Action recommendations

## How It Works

Guest Feedback
↓
NLP Analysis
↓
Sentiment Detection
↓
Urgency & Risk Scoring
↓
Smart Department Routing
↓
Resolution
↓
Escalation if Required
